# Are Official Forecasts Pricing In AI?
## Evidence from BLS Occupational Projections, 2021–2035

**Analysis protocol v1.0 | Prepared November 18, 2026 | GitHub submission pending**

### Question and scope

Do rolling revisions to BLS occupational projections and documented AI factors align with usage-based exposure, conditional on theoretical exposure? This is a descriptive study of official projections, not a causal estimate of AI employment effects or evidence that BLS used a particular metric. Five vintages span 2021–31 through 2025–35; 831 consistently mapped occupations form the comparison universe. The primary complete-case sample has 641 occupations. Later usage measures provide retrospective comparisons with earlier forecasts. Neither the first transition nor the industry component is assumed to be an untreated placebo.

### Measurement

For wage-and-salary employment, define E* = sum_i(E_i,T × s_oi,0), G = log(E_T/E_0)/10, I = log(E*/E_0)/10, and U = log(E_T/E*)/10. Thus G = I + U and their revisions add exactly. Use the verified 224-group common industry partition, with 20 sectors as sensitivity. U includes composition changes within retained aggregate industry groups; it is not a pure analyst-adjustment measure. Self-employment is outside this decomposition. Preserve suppressed or unpublished cells and rounding intervals; never fill them with zero or a chosen feasible completion. Published total-growth revisions have separately identified point estimates. Full-population I/U results use conservative coefficient ranges, not midpoint outcomes, sharp bounds, or confidence intervals.

### Primary specification

Estimate a common weighted linear-projection design for each of four adjacent revisions in G, I and U: observed exposure, theoretical exposure, log baseline employment, wage controls, education indicators and SOC major-group fixed effects. Fix 2021 wage-and-salary employment weights and controls across transitions. May 2021 wages were released March 31, 2022; education values were independently observed by September 22, 2022. Use the uncensored log-wage term plus separate censoring/missing indicators, without treating censored wages as exact. The reconstructed theory dimension is the median of Felten, Eloundou and Eisfeldt ranks; observed exposure is the mean of Anthropic and Microsoft ranks. The Eloundou component is the median of six source-specific ranks. These are reconstructed measures, not BLS internal scores. Coefficients report annual log percentage points per ten exposure-percentile points. Conditional on fixed X and weights, compute signed linear extrema over outcome intervals, then intersect with the additive coefficient identity. These ranges omit exposure, mapping and weight uncertainty.

### Documentary outcomes and inference

Human-review all 5,145 Table 1.12 records under the frozen coding protocol before constructing indicators. The 218 keyword hits only prioritize review. Code general AI, explicit GenAI, direction, mechanisms and supporting source excerpts; unresolved cases remain blank. A zero means no qualifying mention in the available fully reviewed documentation, not no internal consideration. The latest-vintage general-AI indicator is the primary documentary outcome; explicit GenAI is secondary. Use linear probability models with the same design. For valid point outcomes, report SOC minor-cluster CR1 standard errors and t reference intervals with clusters minus one degrees of freedom; these are working-model reference calculations. The primary sample has 91 clusters. Do not fit constant documentary outcomes or infer significance from degenerate residuals. Apply Holm to the four primary observed-exposure G-revision tests plus the latest general-AI test, only when all five are defined in one combined run.
